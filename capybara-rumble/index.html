Spend snacks to send capybaras left and topple the predator den. Win stages to earn berries, then open Capy Crates for new capybaras. Keys: 1–9 and 0 send units, W upgrades the stash, G fires the geyser, F fullscreen, K keyboard mode.

Keyboard mode: ← → (or A / D) move the yellow highlight, Space or Enter uses it. 1–9 and 0 send units, W stash, G geyser, P pause, F fullscreen, K leaves keyboard mode. In menus, arrows pick and Enter chooses.
