# Tiny Realms: 2D low-poly rebuild

## What and why
John asked (in claude.ai) for Tiny Realms to go back to 2D in a low-poly style, with the real Polytopia tech tree, clearer move squares, bigger buttons at the bottom, world sizes and types, a recap of what enemy tribes did, and the classic Cymanti look alongside New Dawn. This replaces the 3D copy that was in `tiny-realms/`.

## Details
- Tech tree now follows the Polytopia wiki (Burn Forest under Construction, Destroy under Chivalry, Starfish under Navigation, Philosophy starts Ai-mo-style tribe, Cymanti-style tribe cannot build ports and uses Foam).
- Normal units are drawn cube-style; the bug tribe has three skins (classic, New Dawn pink, New Dawn blue).
- World sizes: Tiny 11 to Massive 30. World types: Drylands, Lakes, Pangea, Continents, Archipelago, Water World.
- Tested: headless AI-vs-AI games on every world type (8 and 15 tribes), and full pass-and-play games in a phone-sized browser with no errors.
- Home-screen block (manifest, icons, service worker) kept; `sw.js` cache bumped to v3 so iPads pick up the new version.

## Done when
John has played it on an iPad; nothing needed from Desktop.
