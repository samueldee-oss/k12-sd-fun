# New game: Blade Mosaic

## What and why
The user asked for a game where you are a spinning blade that demolishes block mosaics shaped like things (avocado, Eiffel Tower...), plus a blade shop. It is in `blade-mosaic/index.html` (one file, no build) and on the dashboard.

## Details
- 11 mosaics in order: Heart, Apple, Mushroom, Avocado, Fish, Ice Cream, Pizza, Cactus, Rocket, Eiffel Tower, Castle. Each one unlocks the next. Tough blocks (avocado pit, tower iron, castle stone) take 2.5x the hits.
- Coins come from blocks (combo multiplier up to x4), a finish bonus and a star bonus. Stars depend on time (the 3-star time is shown at the end).
- Shop: 8 blades, from Rusty Saw (free) to Galaxy Vortex (14,000 coins). Each one has power, reach, speed and cooling.
- Systems hook: a **heat meter**. Cutting heats the blade, and an overheated blade stops cutting until it cools (balancing loop). The shop is the reinforcing loop (coins → better blade → faster grinding → more coins). Both are explained in a "Systems corner" box in the shop.
- Controls: the blade follows the mouse, or drag anywhere on iPad (relative drag, so your finger never covers the blade), or arrows/WASD. P/Esc pauses. Progress is saved in the browser (localStorage).
- Tested: headless Chromium, no console errors; a bot played all 11 levels in a row, buying the best blade it could afford; a phone-size touch drag moved the blade and cut blocks. The bot played perfectly, earned enough by the end for the Flame Wheel (5th of 8 blades), and got 3 stars everywhere, so real students will be slower.
