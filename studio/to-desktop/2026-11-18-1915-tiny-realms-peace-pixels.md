# Tiny Realms: Lighthouses, peace treaties, pixel units

## What and why
John asked for Lighthouses, peace that actually works, and cuter pixel-style units (including all Cymanti looks), plus Flowers for the New Dawn bug tribe.

## Details
- Lighthouses sit in the 4 map corners; finding one reveals the area and gives 3 stars; finding all 4 gives 10 more.
- Tribes button: offer peace (needs Strategy), declare war, build embassies (needs Diplomacy, +1 income for both sides). At peace, tribes cannot attack each other or take each other's cities. AI tribes offer and accept peace, and may break it.
- Units are drawn as small pixel sprites with outlines; people are cube-style with cute faces.
- Bug tribe: Farming gives Flowers (New Dawn) or Fungi (classic) instead of farms.
- Tested: headless AI games on several world types (8 tribes) and full pass-and-play games in a phone-sized browser, no errors.

John has tried it; nothing needed from Desktop.
